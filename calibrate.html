Step 0 · Calibration

WebGazer has to learn what your eyes look like before it can estimate where you are looking.

  1. Sit about an arm's length from the screen, face lit from the front. Keep your head still from now on.
  2. Optional: press V to see the camera preview and check your face is inside the box, then press V again to hide it.
  3. 9 red dots will appear. Look straight at a dot and click it 5 times while looking at it. It turns green when done.

Allow camera access when asked. The video stays in this browser.

Calibration done

Look around the screen. The red gaze dot should roughly follow your eyes. If it's far off, recalibrate.
